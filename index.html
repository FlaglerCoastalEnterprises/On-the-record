Launch kit · Ready when you are — nothing posted yet

How people get in: one link.

Yes — exactly like a link. When you share this page from Spaces, people tap the link and it opens in their browser. No app, no download, no account. Share the page link for the whole county, or open any building above and tap Share this building — that link lands them straight on that building. Post the link on Facebook, text it, email it, or put it in a YouTube description — it's the same link everywhere.

1 · Launch post — copy & paste

Flagler County government, on a map. 🗺️ We built the On The Record: tap a building — City Hall, the courthouse, the pier, the YMCA site — and see what was decided there, what's next, and when, in plain English. Gold pins had news this week. Start with Palm Coast City Hall or the Government Services Building and explore from there. 👇 Tap the link, tap a building, tell us what you think.

2 · Hot-news post template

[What just happened] at [Building] — [one plain-English sentence on the decision and the dollar figure]. See what it means, what's next, and the timeline — on the map 👇 [Paste that building's Share link here]

Use this the day a pin lights up — Bunnell wastewater hearing Nov 23, Amendment 3 in December, the YMCA decision. Always share the building link, not just the homepage.

3 · 45-second demo video script

  1. 0–5s: Globe on screen. “What if county government had a map?”
  2. 5–15s: Tap the globe — fly into Flagler. “Every public building. Gold means news this week.”
  3. 15–30s: Tap Palm Coast City Hall. “New council, two budget votes — in plain English.”
  4. 30–40s: Tap Share this building. “Send one building to anyone, with one link.”
  5. 40–45s: “On The Record — link below. Follow the buildings you care about.”

Film it as a screen recording on your phone — no face on camera needed.

4 · For realtors & new residents

Moving to Flagler County? Watch your neighborhood before you buy: what's being built, what's being taxed, and what's being decided — building by building, on one map. Free, in plain English, from a Palm Coast company.

Send the page link with this blurb to 5–10 local realtors as a resource they can forward to clients.

5 · Go-live checklist

  • Load a second, fresh week of news
  • Share the page from Spaces to get the public link
  • Test the link in a private browser window + on a phone
  • Test one building Share link end-to-end
  • Post the launch post on the Facebook Page
  • Help answer one hot thread in a local group — with the sourced building link
  • Send the realtor blurb to your first 5 contacts

6 · What success looks like

Count four things each week: link taps per post, how many visitors open a second building, Follow emails received per building, and shares by people you don't know. If a hot-news post can't move those, more posting won't fix it — better news timing will.

How it works · First visit

Explore Flagler County, building by building

Every public building has its own page — what happened there this week, what’s next, and when. You don’t need to read a whole agenda to follow along.

  1. 1
    Tap a pin on the map

    Gold pins had news this week. Gray pins were quiet. Tapping a pin opens that building’s card beside the map.

  2. 2
    Search or filter to find your topic

    Use the search box for a building, a board, or a topic — try “YMCA” or “animal control” — or tap a layer like Meetings, Money, or Schools. The map flies to your match.

  3. 3
    Read the card, check what’s next

    Each card gives the decision in plain English, the next meeting or deadline, and a short timeline — plus Share and Follow for that one building.
